Charter & Evidence
How this site answers the DIG Statement of Purpose and Priorities, and the accreditation evidence it produces.

Purpose. DIG promotes shared accountability for data across key administrative stakeholders. It is a working committee that identifies data integrity challenges, recommends solutions, and implements action plans so data is collected, entered, and used consistently for institutional planning and reporting.

Scope. Data collection, analysis, security, and distribution, across two areas: data management (infrastructure & pipelines) and data analytics (insights & measurements).
